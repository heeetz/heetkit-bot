# HeetKit v1.5.6

Analytics now shows the most active chatters for the current or last stream and
the current calendar month. Monthly totals reset at 00:00 UTC on the first day of
the month. Use `!top` for the monthly leaders and `!rank` for your own place.
You can exclude bot accounts from the leaderboards in Analytics.

## Improvements and fixes

- **Logs** opens recent retained entries faster.
- **Twitch setup and Getting started** provide clearer guidance and remember when
  you skip optional AI setup. Analytics navigation and command breakdown are refined.
- **Editors** for Commands, AI and Filters keep a consistent dialog height.
- **Validation** now checks affected areas earlier and provides better failure
  diagnostics for development builds.

## Updating

Installed copies of v1.5.5 can use **About > Update & restart** once v1.5.6 is
available. For a manual install, back up your profile and exit HeetKit from the
tray before running Setup. Portable copies update by downloading the new ZIP.

The built-in commands reserve `top` and `rank`. Existing custom commands or
aliases using those names can lose their triggers; review them after updating.
